About Toolkit

Toolkit is a collection of free online utilities built on a simple belief: everyday digital tasks should not require accounts, subscriptions, or surrendering your privacy.

Every tool on this site runs entirely in your web browser. When you compress an image or generate a QR code, the processing happens on your own device — your files are never uploaded to our servers, because we do not operate any servers that receive them.

What we offer

  • Image compression (JPG/PNG to WebP or JPEG)
  • QR code generation
  • Secure password generation
  • Word and character counting
  • Age calculation and unit conversion

How we stay free

Toolkit is supported by advertising. We show ads from Google AdSense, which helps cover hosting costs while keeping every tool free forever. We do not sell data, and we do not track you beyond what is described in our Privacy Policy.
