How it works

Select any JPG or PNG image, choose a quality level and output format, and the tool compresses it instantly using your browser's built-in canvas engine. Large photos are automatically resized to a maximum of 1920px on the longest side — ideal for web uploads and messaging apps.

Why WebP?

WebP images are typically 25–35% smaller than JPEG at the same visual quality, and are supported by all modern browsers, WhatsApp and most upload forms.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. Compression happens locally in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server.

What is the maximum file size?

There is no hard limit, but very large images (50MP+) may be slow on older phones.
