Privacy Policy

Ham kya data lete hain?

Bahut kam. Toolkit par koi account nahi banta aur hamare server par tumhara koi data store nahi hota. Zyadatar tools tumhare browser me hi chalte hain — jaise image compressor me tumhari photo kabhi upload nahi hoti.

Browser me kya save hota hai?

  • Bhasha ki pasand — Hindi/English/Hinglish ka chunav tumhare browser me save rehta hai
  • Saved tools — pasandida tools ki soochi bhi sirf tumhare browser me rehti hai

Ye jankari kabhi hamare paas nahi aati aur browser data saaf karne par hat jati hai.

Analytics aur vigyapan

  • Google Analytics — site ko behtar banane ke liye gumnam istemal ke aankde ikattha karta hai, jaise kitne log kaun-sa tool istemal karte hain. Isme naam ya email jaisi pehchan wali jankari nahi hoti.
  • Vigyapan — agar bhavishya me vigyapan (jaise Google AdSense) lagaye gaye, to Google vigyapan dikhane ke liye cookies istemal kar sakta hai. Tum apne browser setting se cookies band kar sakte ho.

Bahari link

Hamari AI tools directory aur kuch tools (jaise AI chat) bahari sevaon se judte hain. Un sites ki apni privacy policy hoti hai — niji ya bank jankari wahan na dalen.
