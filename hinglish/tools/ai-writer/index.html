AI Writer

Shayari, kahani, nibandh — AI likh dega

Tool box

Kaise istemal karen

  1. Dropdown se mode chuno — shayari, kahani, nibandh, caption ya sudhar
  2. Vishay ya text wale box me topic likho
  3. Likhwao dabao — natija aate hi copy karo

Features

  • 5 mode — shayari, kahani, nibandh, caption, text sudhar
  • Hindi aur English dono me likhe
  • Ek click me poora text copy
  • Social media caption mode
  • Poori tarah free, bina signup

Aksar puche jaane wale sawal

Kya AI writer free hai?

Haan, poori tarah free hai. Bina signup jitna chaho likhwao.

Kaun-kaun se mode hain?

5 mode hain — shayari, chhoti kahani, nibandh, social media caption aur text sudhar.

Kya Hindi me shayari likhega?

Haan, Hindi aur English dono me likhta hai. Shayari ke liye vishay saaf likho, jaise barish par shayari.

Likha hua text kitna bharosemand hai?

AI achha draft deta hai, lekin tathyon wali jankari (tarikh, naam, aankde) khud janch lena behtar hai.

Kya main ise school ya college ke kaam me istemal kar sakta hu?

Idea aur draft ke liye haan, lekin apna kaam khud jancho aur apne shabdon me dhalo — seedhe copy-paste se bachna behtar hai.
