Aksar puche jaane wale sawal

Kaun-si ikaaiyaan badli jaa sakti hain?

Lambai (km, m, cm, mile, feet, inch...), vazan (kg, gram, pound, ounce...) aur taapmaan (°C, °F, K) — har shreni me kai ikaaiyaan hain.

Taapmaan ka hisaab kaise hota hai?

Standard formulon se — jaise °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32. Taapmaan seedhe anupaat se nahi, formule se badalta hai.

Nateeja kitna sateek hai?

Dashamlav tak sateek vaigyaanik conversion factor istemal hote hain — rozmarra aur padhai dono ke kaam ke liye kaafi.

Kya internet chahiye?

Page ek baar load hone ke baad nahi — saara hisaab aapke browser me hi hota hai.

Kya ye free hai?

Haan, poori tarah free — koi sign-up nahi.
