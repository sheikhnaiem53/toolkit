Aksar puche jaane wale sawal

Kya AI chat free hai?

Haan, poori tarah free hai. Koi signup ya API key nahi chahiye.

Jawab aane me kitna samay lagta hai?

Aam taur par kuch second. Free seva hone se kabhi-kabhi thoda zyada samay lag sakta hai.

Kya main Hindi me puch sakta hu?

Haan, Hindi aur English dono me pucho — AI dono samajhta hai.

Kya meri chat private hai?

Chat aapke browser me dikhti hai, lekin jawab free AI seva se aata hai — isliye bank, password ya niji jankari yahan na likhen.

AI galat jawab de to?

AI kabhi-kabhi galti kar sakta hai. Zaroori jankari (sehat, paisa, kanoon) kisi vishwasniya srot se zaroor janchen.
