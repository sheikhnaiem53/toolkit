Kaise istemal karen

Features

  • Vazan ÷ lambai² ke standard formula se sateek BMI
  • WHO ki 4 health category (kam/samanya/zyada/motapa)
  • Aapki lambai ke hisaab se sahi vazan range
  • Aapka data browser se bahar nahi jaata

Aksar puche jaane wale sawal

BMI kaise nikaala jaata hai?

BMI = vazan (kg) ÷ lambai (m)². Jaise 65 kg aur 1.7 m par BMI lagbhag 22.5 hota hai.

BMI category kya hain?

WHO ke hisaab se: 18.5 se kam — kam vazan, 18.5–24.9 — samanya, 25–29.9 — zyada vazan, 30 ya zyada — motapa.

Kya BMI sabke liye sahi hai?

Khilaadiyon, garbhavati mahilaon aur buzurgon ke liye BMI bhramak ho sakta hai, kyunki ye maanspeshi aur charbi me farq nahi karta. Ye sirf shuruaati jaanch hai.

Kya ye doctor ki salaah ki jagah le sakta hai?

Nahi. Ye tool sirf jaankari ke liye hai. Vazan ya sehat se juda koi faisla doctor se salaah lekar hi lein.

Kya mera data save hota hai?

Nahi, ganana poori tarah aapke browser me hoti hai. Aapki lambai-vazan kahin upload nahi hoti.
