Text to Speech

Text likho aur awaaz me suno — Hindi aur English

Tool box

Kaise istemal karen

  1. Text wale box me sunne wala text likho
  2. Dropdown se awaaz chuno aur speed set karo
  3. Suno dabao — rokne ke liye roko dabao

Features

  • Hindi aur English dono me bole
  • Kai awaazon me se chunne ki suvidha
  • Speed control (0.5x se 2x tak)
  • Pehle browser me chale — private
  • Browser me awaaz na ho to online vikalp

Aksar puche jaane wale sawal

Kya ye free hai?

Haan, poori tarah free hai. Koi signup nahi, koi seema nahi.

Hindi awaaz kyun nahi aa rahi?

Apne phone ki bhasha setting me Hindi TTS chalu karen. Android par settings me bhasha ke andar Google TTS ki Hindi awaaz download karen.

Kya mera text kahin bheja jata hai?

Pehle ye aapke browser ki built-in awaaz istemal karta hai — tab kuch bhi bahar nahi jata. Agar browser me awaaz na ho to online awaaz ke liye text internet se guzarta hai.

Kya bina internet chalega?

Agar aapke browser ya phone me built-in awaaz hai to haan, bina internet bhi chalega. Online vikalp ke liye internet chahiye.

Speed kaise badlu?

Speed slider ko 0.5x (dheema) se 2x (tez) tak set karo — sunne se pehle ya baad me kabhi bhi.
