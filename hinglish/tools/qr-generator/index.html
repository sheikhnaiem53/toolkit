QR Code Generator

Link, number ya text ka QR code banao aur download karo.

Tool box

Kaise istemal karen

  1. Text/link wale box me apna link ya text likho.
  2. QR Banao button dabao — QR turant dikhega.
  3. Download dabaakar QR ko PNG image ke roop me save karo.

Features

  • Link, phone number, text — kuch bhi QR me badlo
  • Ek click me PNG image download
  • QR ki koi expiry nahi — hamesha kaam karega
  • Aapka data kahin save nahi hota — sab browser me banta hai

Aksar puche jaane wale sawal

QR me kya-kya daala jaa sakta hai?

Website link, phone number, koi text, email — jo bhi scan karne par dikhe ya khule, wahi QR me encode ho jaata hai.

Kya QR code ki expiry hoti hai?

Nahi. QR sirf aapka text/link rakhta hai, isliye ye hamesha kaam karega — jab tak link khud chal raha hai.

Kya mera data kahin upload hota hai?

Nahi. QR aapke browser me hi banta hai; aapka link ya text kisi server par nahi bheja jaata.

Download kis format me hota hai?

PNG image ke roop me — jise print, visiting card ya poster me aasaani se istemal kar sakte ho.

Kya ye free hai?

Haan, poori tarah free — koi sign-up ya QR banaane ki limit nahi.
