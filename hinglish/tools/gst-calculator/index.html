Kaise istemal karen

  1. Raashi (₹) daalo aur GST dar (5/12/18/28%) chuno.
  2. GST Jodo ya GST Ghatao me se tareeka chuno.
  3. Mool raashi, GST raashi aur kul apne-aap dekho.

Features

  • Saare Bharatiya GST slab: 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%
  • GST jodne aur ghataane (reverse) dono mode
  • Mool raashi, tax aur kul ka saaf breakup
  • Billing aur quotation ke turant hisaab ke liye

Aksar puche jaane wale sawal

GST Ghatao ka matlab kya hai?

Agar keemat me GST pehle se shaamil hai, to ye mode asli mool keemat aur usme chhipa GST alag-alag nikaal deta hai — ise reverse calculation kehte hain.

Kaun-se GST slab supported hain?

Bharat ke chaaron mukhya slab — 5%, 12%, 18% aur 28%. Dar wala dropdown se chunein.

Kya ye tax salaah hai?

Nahi, ye sirf turant anumaan ke liye hai. GST filing ya kanooni maamlon ke liye apne CA se salaah lein.

Kya mera data save hota hai?

Nahi, saara hisaab aapke browser me hota hai — koi raashi kahin upload nahi hoti.

Kya ye free hai?

Haan, poori tarah free — koi sign-up nahi.
