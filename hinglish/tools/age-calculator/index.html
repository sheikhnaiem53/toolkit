Kaise istemal karen

  1. Janm tithi wale box me apni date of birth chuno.
  2. Umar Nikalo button dabao.
  3. Saal-mahine-din me umar, kul din aur janm ka din dekho.

Features

  • Saal, mahine aur din me bilkul sahi umar
  • Kul mahino aur kul dino ki ginti
  • Janm ka din (saptah ka kaun-sa vaar) bataata hai
  • Leap year ko dhyaan me rakhkar sateek ganana

Aksar puche jaane wale sawal

Umar ki ganana kaise hoti hai?

Aaj ki tareekh se aapki janm tithi ghataakar saal, mahine aur din nikaale jaate hain. Leap year (29 February) ka bhi sahi hisaab rakha jaata hai.

Kya meri janm tithi kahin save hoti hai?

Nahi. Ganana aapke browser me hi hoti hai aur aapki janm tithi kisi server par nahi bheji jaati.

Agar mujhe sahi janm tithi yaad na ho to?

Andazan tareekh daalkar bhi umar ka anumaan mil jaayega, lekin sarkaari form ke liye hamesha sahi janm tithi hi istemal karein.

Ye tool kis kaam aata hai?

Pariksha aur naukri ki umar-seema check karne, form me saal-mahine wali umar bharne, beema aur pension ki yogyata jaan-ne me.

Kya ye free hai?

Haan, poori tarah free — koi sign-up ya payment nahi.
