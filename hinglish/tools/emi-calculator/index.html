Features

  • Slider se raashi (₹50 hazaar–₹2 crore), dar aur avadhi chuno

Aksar puche jaane wale sawal

EMI kaise nikaali jaati hai?

Ghat-te balance (reducing balance) wale standard formula se — usi tareeke se jaise bank hisaab lagaate hain.

Kya isme processing fee ya GST shaamil hai?

Nahi. Ye sirf mooldhan aur byaaj ka hisaab hai. Asli laagat me bank ki fee aur charge alag se judte hain.

Ye nateeja kitna bharosemand hai?

Ye ek anumaan hai, vittiya salaah nahi. Loan lene se pehle asli darein aur shartein apne bank se zaroor confirm karein.

EMI kam karne ke kya tareeke hain?

Zyada down payment, lambi avadhi (byaaj badhega), ya kam byaaj dar wala loan chun-ne se maasik kist ghat-ti hai.

Kya ye free hai?

Haan, poori tarah free — koi sign-up nahi.
