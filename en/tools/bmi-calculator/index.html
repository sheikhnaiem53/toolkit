How to use

  1. Enter your height (cm) and weight (kg) in the boxes.
  2. Press the Calculate BMI button.
  3. See your BMI, category and healthy weight range.

Features

  • Accurate BMI using the standard weight ÷ height² formula
  • All 4 WHO health categories (under/normal/over/obese)
  • Healthy weight range for your height
  • Your data never leaves the browser

Frequently asked questions

How is BMI calculated?

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². For example, 65 kg at 1.7 m gives a BMI of about 22.5.

What are the BMI categories?

Per WHO: below 18.5 — underweight, 18.5–24.9 — normal, 25–29.9 — overweight, 30 or more — obese.

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

It can mislead for athletes, pregnant women and older adults, because it doesn't distinguish muscle from fat. It's only a first screening step.

Can this replace a doctor's advice?

No. This tool is for information only. Always consult a doctor before making any weight or health decision.

Is my data saved?

No, the calculation runs entirely in your browser. Your height and weight are never uploaded.
