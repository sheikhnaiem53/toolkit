How to use

  1. Tap choose photo and select your image
  2. Pick a quality level and WebP/JPEG format
  3. Check the result and hit download

Features

  • Large photos auto-resized to 1920px
  • WebP format — 25 to 35% smaller than JPEG
  • Quality slider for size control
  • See before/after size and savings
  • 100% private — no uploads

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Compression happens entirely in your browser — your files never leave your phone or computer.

What is WebP and why choose it?

WebP is a modern image format that is 25 to 35% smaller than JPEG at nearly the same quality. It is the best choice for WhatsApp and websites.

What quality should I pick?

80% is right for most uses. 60 to 70% is fine for forms or WhatsApp; use over 90% for printing.

How large a photo can I add?

There is no strict limit, but very large photos are auto-resized to 1920px to keep the file light.

Is it free?

Yes, completely free with no signup. Compress as many photos as you like.
