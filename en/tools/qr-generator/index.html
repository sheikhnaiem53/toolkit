QR Code Generator

Create QR codes for links, numbers or text — and download them.

Toolbox

How to use

  1. Type your link or text in the text/link box.
  2. Press the Generate QR button — the code appears instantly.
  3. Press Download to save the QR as a PNG image.

Features

  • Turn links, phone numbers or text into QR codes
  • One-click PNG image download
  • QR codes never expire — they work forever
  • Your data is never stored — everything is made in the browser

Frequently asked questions

What can I put in a QR code?

Website links, phone numbers, any text, email — whatever should show or open on scanning gets encoded into the QR.

Do QR codes expire?

No. A QR only holds your text/link, so it works forever — as long as the link itself is live.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The QR is created in your browser; your link or text is never sent to any server.

What format is the download?

As a PNG image — easy to use in prints, visiting cards or posters.

Is it free?

Yes, completely free — no sign-up or limit on QR codes.
