How to use

  1. Type or paste your text into the box above.
  2. Instantly see words, characters, sentences and reading time below.
  3. Every count updates automatically as you edit the text.

Features

  • Live counting as you type — no button press needed
  • Separate character count excluding spaces
  • Estimated reading time in minutes
  • Your text never leaves the browser — fully private

Frequently asked questions

Is this word counter free?

Yes, completely free. No sign-up, login or payment needed — just open the page and start counting.

Is my text saved or uploaded anywhere?

No. All counting happens inside your browser. Your text is never sent to any server, so even private documents stay safe.

How are words counted?

Every part separated by spaces counts as one word. Multiple spaces or line breaks don't break the count.

How is reading time calculated?

It's estimated from an average reading speed (about 200 words per minute). It's only an estimate — your actual time depends on your pace.

Can it count long articles?

Yes, even thousands of words are counted instantly. Since everything runs in the browser, large texts cause no delay.
