How to use

  1. Choose a category: length, weight or temperature.

Features

  • Common pairs like km–miles, kg–pounds, °C–°F
  • Instant result as you type
  • Runs fully in your browser — no server needed

Frequently asked questions

Which units can be converted?

Length (km, m, cm, miles, feet, inches...), weight (kg, grams, pounds, ounces...) and temperature (°C, °F, K) — each category has many units.

How is temperature converted?

With standard formulas — e.g. °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32. Temperature converts by formula, not by simple ratio.

How accurate is the result?

Precise scientific conversion factors are used, down to decimals — enough for daily use and studies.

Do I need the internet?

Not after the page loads once — all math happens in your browser.

Is it free?

Yes, completely free — no sign-up.
