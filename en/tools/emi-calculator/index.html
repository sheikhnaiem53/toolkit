How to use

  • Results update instantly as you move sliders
  • Works for home, car, personal — any loan type

Frequently asked questions

How is EMI calculated?

Using the standard reducing-balance formula — the same method banks use to compute your instalment.

Does it include processing fees or GST?

No. This covers only principal and interest. Bank fees and charges add to the real cost separately.

How reliable is this result?

It's an estimate, not financial advice. Always confirm actual rates and terms with your bank before taking a loan.

How can I lower my EMI?

A bigger down payment, a longer tenure (interest rises), or a loan with a lower interest rate all reduce the monthly instalment.

Is it free?

Yes, completely free — no sign-up.
