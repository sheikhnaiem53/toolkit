How to use

  1. Pick your calculation: Y% of X, X is what % of Y, or % change.
  2. Enter both numbers and press =.
  3. The answer appears instantly below.

Features

  • Three modes: Y% of X, percentage share, and % change
  • Everyday math for discounts, marks and growth
  • Decimal numbers supported
  • Instant answer at the press of =

Frequently asked questions

How do I find a discounted price?

In Y% of X mode enter the real price and discount % — you get the discount amount. Subtract it from the price for the final cost.

How do I calculate marks percentage?

Choose the X is what % of Y mode — e.g. 360 out of 450 marks = 80%. Most useful for exam results.

How is increase or decrease % found?

In % change mode enter the old and new numbers — e.g. a price rising from ₹500 to ₹650 shows +30%.

Are decimal numbers allowed?

Yes, decimal values like 12.5% are fully supported.
