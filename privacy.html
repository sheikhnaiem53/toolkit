Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. Data we do NOT collect

All tools on Toolkit run entirely in your web browser. Images you compress, text you count, and passwords you generate are processed locally on your device and are never transmitted to us. We operate no user accounts and store no personal files.

2. Data we may collect

  • Contact messages: if you email us, we receive your email address and message content, used only to reply.
  • Anonymous analytics: we may use privacy-friendly analytics to understand aggregate usage (pages visited, device type). This data cannot identify you.

3. Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Google may use cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve personalised ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings. Learn more in Google's advertising policy.

4. Third-party resources

Some tools load open-source libraries from public CDNs (e.g. a QR code library). Those providers may log standard request data per their own policies.

5. Children's privacy

Toolkit is a general-utility site not directed at children under 13, and we knowingly collect no data from children.

6. Changes

We may update this policy; the latest version will always be posted on this page.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, use our contact page.
